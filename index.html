Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Bullet Journal mobile application ("the App") handles information. The App is a personal productivity and journaling tool developed for Android.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Bullet Journal is designed to work entirely offline for your personal data. All of your entries, tasks, notes, journal pages, and habits are stored locally on your device only, using a local database. We do not operate any server, account system, or cloud backend, and we do not collect, transmit, or have access to:

  • Your journal entries, tasks, notes, or habit data
  • Your name, email address, or any account information (the App does not require account creation or sign-in)
  • Your location
  • Photos, contacts, or any other personal files on your device

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to use and supported by advertising provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect certain technical information, such as:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • General device and network information
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed directly by Google, not by the App's developer. For details, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services

Depending on your region, you may be shown a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) allowing you to manage your ad personalization preferences the first time you open the App.

3. Permissions Used by the App

  • Internet / Network State - used exclusively to load and display ads, and to detect connectivity. No core feature of the App requires an internet connection to be created or viewed.
  • Notifications - used only to show local reminders you set for tasks, habits, or your daily log. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are never sent to or from a server.
  • Receive Boot Completed - used only to reschedule your local reminders after your device restarts.

The App does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, storage, or precise location.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal journal data with anyone, because it never leaves your device in the first place. The only third party involved in the App is Google AdMob, as described in Section 2.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. You can permanently delete all app data at any time from the App's Settings screen ("Delete all data"), or by clearing the App's storage/data from your device's system settings, or by uninstalling the App.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
